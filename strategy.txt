3) Strategy: WeatherGuard serves institutions in "extreme weather regions" meaning the
rules for what constitutes acceptable attendance will vary by weather event and
preference. Currently, attendance determination and the temperature unit toggle
(setTemperatureUnit(boolean) in WeatherService) are both handled with inline
conditionals. The Strategy pattern defines a family of interchangeable algorithms,
encapsulates each one, and makes them swappable without changing the context object.
Two concrete application areas exist immediately. First, an AttendanceRuleStrategyIF
with implementations like StrictAttendanceStrategy (no grace period),
GracePeriodStrategy (10-minute window), and WeatherLeniencyStrategy (extended
window when outdoor temp < 32°F or wind > 40 mph) would let administrators
configure per-class or per-weather policies without touching the controller. Second, the
temperature unit preference already in WeatherService can be formalized as a
TemperatureDisplayStrategyIF with FahrenheitStrategy and CelsiusStrategy
implementations, replacing the boolean flag with a polymorphic call. Both usages share
the same structural pattern: the context holds a reference to a StrategyIF, and swapping
the reference changes the behavior at runtime without any if/else branching.
